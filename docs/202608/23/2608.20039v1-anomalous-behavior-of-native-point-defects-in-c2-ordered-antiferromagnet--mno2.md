## 摘要
α-MnO₂因其结构灵活性和缺陷驱动的功能性，正成为一种用于电子、光电子和能源应用的新兴材料。在α-MnO₂的合成过程中，本征氧空位容易形成，并通常被外来掺杂剂补偿。因此，透彻理解本征缺陷对于实现受控的外来掺杂和优化材料性能至关重要。利用密度泛函方法，我们研究了在本征点缺陷（包括间隙原子、空位和反位）存在下，基态C2型反铁磁α-MnO₂的结构、电子、磁性和光学性质。我们计算了它们的热力学稳定性，并加入了静电修正以消除虚假的长程相互作用。Mn间隙原子（Mnᵢ）和Mn反位O（Mn_O）引入浅施主能级，而O空位（V_O）表现出两性行为并充当补偿中心。计算得到的缺陷形成能揭示了施主型和受主型本征缺陷之间的显著竞争，导致在富Mn和富O生长条件下均存在强烈的本征缺陷补偿。Mn空位（V_Mn）在带隙范围内保持电离并表现为浅受主，表明其在适当的非平衡生长条件下具有潜在作用，而O反位Mn（O_Mn）形成深受主能级。BSE@G₀W₀计算揭示了化学计量比α-MnO₂中强烈的各向异性光学响应，而本征点缺陷引入了显著的亚带隙激发和增强的介电屏蔽，其中空位产生的影响最大。

## Abstract
$α$-MnO$_2$ is an emerging material for electronic, optoelectronic, and energy applications, owing to its structural flexibility and defect-driven functionality. During synthesis of $α$-MnO$_2$, native oxygen vacancies readily form and are typically compensated by foreign dopants. A thorough understanding of intrinsic defects is therefore essential for enabling controlled extrinsic doping and optimizing material performance. Using density functional approach, we investigate the structural, electronic, magnetic, and optical properties of the ground state C2-type antiferromagnetic $α$-MnO$_2$ in the presence of native point defects, including interstitials, vacancies, and antisites. We compute their thermodynamic stability, incorporating electrostatic corrections to eliminate spurious long-range interactions. Mn interstitial (Mn$_\text{i}$) and Mn antisite O (Mn$_\text{O}$) introduce shallow donor levels, whereas O-vacancy (V$_\text{O}$) exhibit amphoteric behavior and act as compensating centers. The calculated defect formation energies reveal pronounced competition between donor- and acceptor-type native defects, leading to strong intrinsic defect compensation under both Mn-rich and O-rich growth conditions. Mn vacancy (V$_\text{Mn}$) remains ionized across the band gap and behaves as a shallow acceptor, suggesting its potential role under suitable non-equilibrium growth conditions, whereas O antisite Mn (O$_\text{Mn}$) forms deep acceptor levels. BSE@G$_0$$W_0$ calculations reveal a strongly anisotropic optical response in stoichiometric $α$-MnO$_2$, while native point defects introduce pronounced sub-gap excitations and enhanced dielectric screening, with vacancies producing the largest effect.
