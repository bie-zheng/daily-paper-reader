## 摘要
我们为非凸、状态及历史依赖动力学的耗散演化开发了一个全轨迹松弛框架。该框架自然地源于能量-耗散演化的博弈论表述。通过因果Volterra重构消除了状态-速率一致性，将问题简化为完整速率轨迹上的对角平衡条件。我们建立了一种基于消逝缺陷可逼近性、紧致性和互补半连续性的直接方法。对于一个具有非凸动力学势和局部记忆变量的简化简单剪切模型，我们精确计算了松弛机制。富化状态是完整局部历史上的Young测度。我们明确识别了纯对角缺陷的逐次下半连续包络，刻画了其零集，为每个松弛零构造了纯恢复序列，并通过冻结最优响应时间离散化证明了存在性。由此产生的不同速率群体与不同记忆的共存为动力学剪切带提供了一种简化机制，并自然地与负应变率敏感性和动态应变时效（如Portevin-Le Chatelier效应背后的机制）相联系。

## Abstract
We develop a whole-trajectory relaxation framework for dissipative evolutions with non-convex, state- and history-dependent kinetics. The framework arises naturally from a game-theoretical formulation of energy--dissipation evolution. State--rate consistency is eliminated by causal Volterra reconstruction, reducing the problem to a diagonal equilibrium condition on complete rate trajectories. We establish a direct method based on vanishing-defect approximability, compactness, and complementary semicontinuity. The relaxation mechanism is computed exactly for a reduced simple-shear model with a non-convex kinetic potential and a local memory variable. The enriched state is a Young measure on complete local histories. We identify explicitly the sequential lower-semicontinuous envelope of the pure diagonal defect, characterize its zero set, construct pure recovery sequences for every relaxed zero, and prove existence through a frozen-best-response time discretization. The resulting coexistence of distinct rate populations with distinct memories provides a reduced mechanism for kinetic shear banding and connects naturally with negative strain-rate sensitivity and dynamic strain aging such as underlie the Portevin--Le Chatelier effect.
