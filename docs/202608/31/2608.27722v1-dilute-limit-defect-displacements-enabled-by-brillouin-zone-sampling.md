## 摘要
缺陷及其与半导体主晶格的相互作用在多种技术中起着至关重要的作用。当缺陷在两个电子态之间跃迁时，晶格会响应性地畸变。大位移发生在缺陷附近，诱发邻近原子位移，并依此类推，在距缺陷数百埃的原子处产生微小位移。由于超胞尺寸有限，第一性原理计算难以精确描述这些位移。在此，我们展示了一种利用典型中等超胞中获取的信息来高效获得稀极限原子位移的方法。在我们的方法中，首先在小超胞中获得原子位移，并在谐波势能面下将其转化为力差。然后，将不同${\bf q}$点处的力差和声子模式展开到Born-von Kármán超胞中，以获得稀极限原子位移。我们严格分析了力差的收敛行为，并研究了可能产生这些力的电子密度差异，认为中等超胞足以处理内部跃迁和束缚激子跃迁。给出了我们方法的两个应用实例：(1)我们计算了金刚石中NV中心和硅中T中心的光致发光光谱；(2)我们获得了这些缺陷的稀极限构型坐标图。特别是，我们发现与声学声子模式的耦合降低了构型坐标图中的接受模式频率。我们的工作为处理缺陷处的真正稀极限跃迁提供了必要的缺失要素。

## Abstract
Defects and their interaction with the semiconductor host lattice play an essential role in a variety of technologies. When a defect transitions between two electronic states, the lattice distorts in response. Large displacements occur near the defect, inducing displacements on neighboring atoms, and so on, producing small displacements on atoms hundreds of Ångströms from the defect. Describing these displacements accurately is challenging for first-principles calculations due to limited supercell sizes. Here we demonstrate a procedure to efficiently obtain dilute-limit atomic displacements, using information obtained in typical modest supercells. In our approach, the atomic displacements are first obtained in a small supercell and converted into a force difference under a harmonic potential energy surface. The force difference and phonon modes at different ${\bf q}$-points are then unfolded into the Born-von Kármán supercell to obtain the dilute-limit atomic displacements. We critically analyze the convergence behavior of the force difference and study possible electron density differences that give rise to those forces, arguing that modest supercells are sufficient for internal transitions and bound-exciton transitions. Two example applications of our approach are given: (1) we calculate the luminescence spectrum of the NV center in diamond and the T center in Si and (2) we obtain dilute-limit configuration coordinate diagrams for these defects. In particular, we find that coupling to acoustic phonon modes reduces the accepting-mode frequency in the configuration coordinate diagram. Our work provides the missing ingredients necessary to address truly dilute-limit transitions at defects.
