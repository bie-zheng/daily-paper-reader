## 摘要
离子沟道和背散射技术是研究晶体材料中晶格无序和缺陷结构的强大工具。然而，沟道现象的准确解释需要利用能够考虑点缺陷、位错和扩展缺陷团簇之间复杂相互作用的模拟模型。本文介绍了一种蒙特卡洛方法，该方法能够在宽范围的分析束能量下重现实验谱，并能够定量识别缺陷类型和分布。模拟揭示了区分点缺陷和扩展缺陷的特征性能量依赖性，为理解例如金属和半导体中离子注入引起的扰动提供了新的视角。为此，开发了McChasy代码，作为科学家灵活且易于使用的工具，能够对各种晶体系统进行建模，包括复杂半导体、多层外延薄膜和氧化物晶体。该程序将离子沟道实验数据与缺陷建模相结合，为材料科学中的缺陷分析建立了一个稳健的框架。本文通过重现先前在铜晶体实验中观察到的沟道谱中的特征性“肘部”，阐述了该程序的模拟能力。

## Abstract
Ion channeling and backscattering techniques are powerful tools for studying crystal lattice disorders and defect structures in crystalline materials. However, the accurate interpretation of channeling phenomena necessitates the utilization of simulation models that account for the intricate interactions between point defects, dislocations, and extended defect clusters. The present paper introduces a Monte Carlo method that reproduces experimental spectra over a wide range of analyzing beam energies and enables quantitative identification of defect types and distributions. The simulations reveal characteristic energy dependencies that distinguish point defects from extended defects, offering a novel perspective on disturbances caused, for example, by ion implantation in metals and semiconductors. To this end, the McChasy code has been developed as a flexible and accessible tool for scientists, enabling the modeling of various crystal systems, including complex semiconductors, multilayer epitaxial films, and oxide crystals. The program's integration of experimental data on ion channeling with defect modeling establishes a robust framework for defect analysis in materials science. The present article expounds upon the simulation capabilities of the program by reproducing the characteristic "elbows" in channeling spectra that were previously observed in experiments conducted on Cu crystals.
