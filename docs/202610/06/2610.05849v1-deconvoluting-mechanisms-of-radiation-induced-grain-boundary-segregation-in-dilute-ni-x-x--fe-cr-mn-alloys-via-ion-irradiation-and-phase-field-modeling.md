## 摘要
Ni 基结构合金中晶界（GBs）处辐照诱导的成分再分布对先进核反应堆的长期部署构成关键挑战。由于在辐照下缺陷介导的输运、热力学驱动力以及相互竞争的微观结构汇均可发挥作用，控制特定溶质偏聚的机制仍认识不足。我们对稀 Ni-Fe、Ni-Cr 和 Ni-Mn 二元合金在 500、800 和 1000 K 下进行系统的 Ni²⁺ 离子辐照至 2 dpa，并利用 STEM-EDS 表征晶界化学成分。偏聚行为通过基于原子尺度输运数据的速率理论相场建模进行解释。我们发现，Fe 在所有温度下均通过优先的空位-溶质交换（逆 Kirkendall 效应）在晶界处贫化。Cr 的偏聚反映了自间隙原子（SIA）介导的向晶界输运与空位介导的离开晶界输运之间随温度变化的竞争，在 500 K 时净偏聚接近零，而在更高温度下表现为贫化。相比之下，Mn 表现出由热力学偏聚主导的独特行为，导致其在晶界处局部富集。我们利用沿同一晶界的深度依赖表征，跨越辐照区和未辐照区，以区分辐射诱导贡献和热力学贡献，并验证未辐照区可作为内部热力学参考。800 K 时的空洞形成以及 800 和 1000 K 时的辐照诱导晶界迁移，通过抑制和重新分布溶质进一步增加了机制复杂性。我们的实验与计算联合评估表明，解读辐照下晶界化学演化需要同时考虑非平衡动力学输运、热力学驱动力、体相汇效应和晶界迁移。

## Abstract
Radiation-induced composition redistribution at grain boundaries (GBs) in Ni-based structural alloys poses a critical challenge for the long-term deployment of advanced nuclear reactors. The mechanisms governing solute-specific segregation remain poorly understood because defect-mediated transport, thermodynamic driving forces, and competing microstructural sinks can all contribute under irradiation. We perform systematic Ni2+ ion irradiations on dilute Ni-Fe, Ni-Cr, and Ni-Mn binary alloys at 500, 800, and 1000 K to 2 dpa and characterize GB chemistry using STEM-EDS. The segregation behavior is interpreted using rate-theory phase-field modeling informed by atomistic transport data. We find that Fe depletes at GBs across all temperatures via preferential vacancy-solute exchange (inverse Kirkendall effect). Cr segregation reflects a temperature-dependent competition between self-interstitial atom (SIA)-mediated transport toward GBs and vacancy-mediated transport away from GBs, yielding near-zero net segregation at 500 K and depletion at higher temperatures. In contrast, Mn exhibits distinct behavior dominated by thermodynamic segregation that drives local enrichment at the GB. We leverage depth-dependent characterization along the same GB, spanning irradiated and unirradiated regions, to separate radiation-induced and thermodynamic contributions and validate the unirradiated region as an internal thermodynamic reference. Void formation at 800 K and irradiation-induced GB migration at 800 and 1000 K further add mechanistic complexity by suppressing and redistributing the solute. Our combined experimental and computational assessments show that interpreting GB chemistry evolution under irradiation requires concurrent treatment of non-equilibrium kinetic transport, thermodynamic driving forces, bulk sink effects, and GB migration.
