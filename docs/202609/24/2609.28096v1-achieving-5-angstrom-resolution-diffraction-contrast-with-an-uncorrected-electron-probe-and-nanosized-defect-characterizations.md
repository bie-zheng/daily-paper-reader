## 摘要
原子分辨率显微图像由于视场小和记录的缺陷数量少，其应用仍然受限。在透射电子显微镜（TEM）中，获得具有可靠统计的缺陷成像在很大程度上依赖于衍射衬度。然而，对于200–300 kV电子，低曲率埃瓦尔德球将衍射分辨率限制在约5 nm。在此，我们在TEM中利用禁戒条件对缺陷成像，并将衍射衬度分辨率从5 nm提高到0.5 nm。我们的equal-s方法的优越性通过发现钢中的一种新相和位错环结构得到证明，这些此前在实验或理论上均未见报道。我们的equal-s方法可在很大程度上替代用于缺陷研究的经典双束条件。

## Abstract
Atomic-resolution micrographs remain limited in application due to small field of view and low numbers of recorded defects. Defect imaging with reliable statistics in transmission electron microscopy (TEM) relies heavily on diffraction contrast. However, with 200-300 kV electrons, low-curvature Ewald sphere limits diffraction resolution at ~5 nm. Here, we imaged defects with a forbidden condition in TEM and improved the resolution in diffraction contrast from 5 to 0.5 nm. The superiority of our equal-s method is demonstrated by the discovery of a novel phase and dislocation loop structure in steels, which have not been previously reported experimentally or theoretically. Our equal-s can largely replace the classical two-beam condition for defect study.
