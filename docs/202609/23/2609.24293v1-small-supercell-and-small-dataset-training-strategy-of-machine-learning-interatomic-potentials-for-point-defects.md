## 摘要
机器学习原子间势（MLIPs）能够以接近第一性原理的精度处理大规模材料体系，并已被广泛用于加速点缺陷模拟。然而，MLIPs 的训练通常依赖大量 DFT 数据。这一问题对带电缺陷尤为突出，因为此类缺陷需要采用大超胞进行 DFT 计算以避免长程库仑相互作用和有限尺寸效应，从而使数据集的构建计算成本高昂。在本工作中，我们提出了一种针对中性及低电荷点缺陷的高效 MLIP 训练方案，该方案基于小超胞（少于 100 个原子）和有限次数的 DFT 计算。该方案仅需四次 DFT 结构弛豫即可构建训练数据集，并且所训练的专用缺陷 MLIP 能够以较小误差（大多小于 0.3 eV）预测更大超胞（超过 200 个原子）中的缺陷形成能。以 GaN、SiO₂ 和 Cu₂ZnSnS₄ 中的缺陷为例，我们评估了该方案在不同超胞尺寸下预测缺陷总能量和结构弛豫的外推能力。结果表明，仅在单个小缺陷超胞上训练的 MLIP 在处理大超胞时会产生严重误差。纳入来自多个超胞尺寸的缺陷数据可提高 MLIP 的预测性能，而加入无缺陷的完美体相超胞则可进一步提升精度。这些结果为以低计算成本训练缺陷体系的 MLIP 模型提供了实用指导。

## Abstract
Machine learning interatomic potentials (MLIPs) can treat large-scale material systems with near first-principles accuracy and have been widely used to accelerate point-defect simulations. However, the training of MLIPs usually relies on large amounts of DFT data. This issue is particularly pronounced for charged defects, for which DFT calculations of large supercells are required to avoid long-range Coulomb interactions and finite-size effects, making the construction of datasets computationally expensive. In this work, we propose an efficient MLIP training scheme for neutral and lowly charged point defects based on small supercells (less than 100 atoms) and limited number of DFT calculations. The scheme requires only four DFT structural relaxations to construct the training dataset and the trained MLIPs dedicated for the defect can predict the defect formation energies in larger supercells (over 200 atoms) with small errors (mostly smaller than 0.3 eV). Using defects in GaN, SiO$_2$, and $\mathrm{Cu}_2\mathrm{ZnSnS}_4$ as representative examples, we evaluate the extrapolation capability of this scheme for predicting defect total energies and structural relaxations across different supercell sizes. The results show that an MLIP trained only on single small defect supercell produces severe errors when treating large supercells. Incorporating defect data from multiple supercell sizes improves the predictive performance of the MLIP, while adding pristine defect-free bulk supercells further enhances the accuracy. These results provide practical guidance for training MLIP models of defect systems with low computational costs.
