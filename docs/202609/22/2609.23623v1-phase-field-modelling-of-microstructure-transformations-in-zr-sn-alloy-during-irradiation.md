## 摘要
在本工作中，我们研究了 Zr-Sn 合金在固溶体热处理阶段以及退火合金在反应堆条件下辐照阶段中溶质和空位浓度空间重排的特性。为此，我们采用相场模拟，并将反应速率理论与 CALPHAD 方法结合在同一个广义框架中。模拟在三维条件下进行，以获得块体中溶质和缺陷重排的详细信息。我们讨论了热处理阶段第二相的出现及其在中子辐照下的稳定性。我们分析了辐照剂量对第二相颗粒统计性质的影响。

## Abstract
In this work we study peculiarities of spatial rearrangement of solute and vacancy concentrations in Zr-Sn alloy at stages of both thermal treatment of solid solution and irradiation of annealed alloy under reactor conditions. To this end, we exploit phase field modelling including reaction rate theory and CALPHAD method combined in one generalized approach. Simulations were done in 3D in order to get a detailed information about rearrangement of solutes and defects in a bulk. We discuss the emergence of the secondary phase at the stage of thermal treatment and its stability under neutron irradiation. We analyze an influence of the irradiation dose onto statistical properties of the secondary phase particles.
