## 摘要
Fe-Cr二元合金是铁素体/马氏体钢的模型体系。Cr团簇引发的延展性丧失是导致合金性能劣化的影响因素之一，其严重程度取决于Cr浓度。本研究旨在通过正电子寿命谱学、透射电子显微镜和穆斯堡尔谱学研究，理解（i）Fe-12Cr合金中Cr析出的性质，以及（ii）比较其与Fe-9Cr合金的析出浓度和尺寸。发现从高温FCC相淬火后的合金含有高密度位错及亚晶结构，导致在748 K淬火后热处理期间形成富Cr的σ相析出；而无位错和亚晶结构制备的合金仅在晶界处出现析出，与Fe-9Cr合金的观察结果一致。选区衍射证实这些析出物是四方金属间化合物σ相，这与多数关于Fe-Cr铁素体合金的文献报道（将其视为富Cr的BCC α'相）相矛盾。穆斯堡尔谱学研究表明基体中Cr贫化的特征，表明析出物相对于合金成分更富集Cr。发现Fe-12Cr合金中Cr析出的动力学高于Fe-9Cr合金。与Fe-9Cr合金相比，Fe-12Cr合金中的平均析出物尺寸更大，而数量密度更低。此外，本文首次通过充分证据报道了Fe-(9, 12)Cr合金无缺陷晶格中的实验正电子寿命。

## Abstract
The Fe-Cr binary alloy is a model system for ferrtitic/martensitic steels. Cr clustering instigated loss of ductility, is one of the influential factors to deteriorate the alloys, and its severity depends on Cr concentration. This study aims at understanding (i) the nature of Cr precipitation in Fe-12Cr alloy using positron lifetime spectroscopy, transmission electron microscopy and Mössbauer spectroscopy studies and (ii) comparing the precipitation concentration and size with respect to Fe-9Cr alloy. The quenched alloys (from the high temperature FCC phase) are found to contain a high density of dislocations along with the sub-grain structure which lead to Cr rich σ-phase precipitation during post-quenching heat treatment at 748 K, while the alloy prepared with no dislocations and the sub-grain structure shows no precipitation, except at grain boundaries, consistent with observations in Fe-9Cr alloys. Selected area diffraction confirmed that the precipitates are tetragonal intermetallic σ-phase, contradicting the majority of the literature on Fe-Cr ferritic alloys which reported it as Cr rich BCC α'-phase. Mossbauer spectroscopy studies showed signatures of Cr depletion from the rest of the matrix, indicating that the precipitates are Cr rich compared to the alloy composition. The kinetics of Cr precipitation is found to be higher in the Fe-12Cr alloy compared to the Fe-9Cr alloy. The average precipitate size is observed to be higher and the number density is lower in the Fe-12Cr alloy compared to the Fe-9Cr alloy. In addition, the experimental positron lifetime in defect-free lattice of Fe-(9, 12)Cr alloys is reported for the first time with substantial evidence.
