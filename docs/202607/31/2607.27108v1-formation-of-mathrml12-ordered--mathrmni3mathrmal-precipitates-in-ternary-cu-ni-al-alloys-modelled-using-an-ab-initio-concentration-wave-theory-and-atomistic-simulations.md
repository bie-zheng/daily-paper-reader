## 摘要
析出强化型Cu-Ni-Al合金因共格、$\mathrm{L}1_2$有序的$γ'$-$\mathrm{Ni}_3\mathrm{Al}$析出相可在赋予材料高机械强度的同时，保留纯Cu特有的许多良好输运性质，因而具有技术应用价值。在本工作中，我们研究了伪二元$\textrm{Cu}_x (\textrm{Ni}_{3/4} \textrm{Al}_{1/4})_{1-x}$体系（$0 \leq x \leq 1$）的热力学与相稳定性。我们采用一种计算建模框架，将第一性原理电子结构计算与浓度波分析相结合，从中提取原子-原子有效对相互作用，用于原子蒙特卡洛模拟。我们的建模揭示了三种不同的、依赖组分的相行为区间，与实验测定的相图定性一致。在低Cu含量下，Cu可溶于$\mathrm{L}1_2$有序的$\mathrm{Ni}_3\mathrm{Al}$相中，存在一个对应于Ni和Al之间化学有序化的单一可辨识相变。在中等组分下，这种高温有序化之后在较低温度下发生Cu与$\mathrm{L}1_2$有序$\mathrm{Ni}_3\mathrm{Al}$的相分离。最后，在高Cu含量下，$\mathrm{L}1_2$有序的$\mathrm{Ni}_3\mathrm{Al}$直接从固溶体中析出，没有明显可辨识的次级转变。我们将这些相变与所研究合金的底层电子结构特征联系起来。总体而言，本工作展示了一种计算高效的工作流程，可同时捕获多组分替代式合金中的化学有序化和共格析出行为，对研究析出强化等现象具有相关性。

## Abstract
Precipitation-strengthened Cu-Ni-Al alloys are of interest for technological applications because coherent, $\mathrm{L}1_2$-ordered $γ'$-$\mathrm{Ni}_3\mathrm{Al}$ precipitates can confer high mechanical strength while allowing the material to retain many of the good transport properties characteristic of elemental Cu. In this work, we study the thermodynamics and phase stability of the pseudobinary $\textrm{Cu}_x (\textrm{Ni}_{3/4} \textrm{Al}_{1/4})_{1-x}$ system, $0 \leq x \leq 1$. We use a computational modelling framework combining first-principles electronic structure calculations with a concentration wave analysis from which atom-atom effective pair interactions are extracted for use in atomistic Monte Carlo simulations. Our modelling reveals three distinct, composition-dependent regimes of phase behaviour, in qualitative agreement with the experimentally determined phase diagram. At low Cu content, Cu is soluble in the $\mathrm{L}1_2$-ordered $\mathrm{Ni}_3\mathrm{Al}$ phase, with a single identifiable phase transition corresponding to chemical ordering between Ni and Al. At intermediate compositions, this high-temperature ordering is followed at lower temperatures by phase separation of Cu and $\mathrm{L}1_2$-ordered $\mathrm{Ni}_3\mathrm{Al}$. Finally, at high Cu content, $\mathrm{L}1_2$-ordered $\mathrm{Ni}_3\mathrm{Al}$ precipitates directly from the solid solution, with no clearly identifiable secondary transition. We relate these phase transformations to features of the underlying electronic structures of the considered alloys. Overall, this work demonstrates a computationally efficient workflow capturing both chemical ordering and coherent precipitation in multicomponent substitutional alloys, with relevance to the study of phenomena such as precipitation strengthening.
